This Privacy Policy explains how Viral Clips Now ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our YouTube channel @GTA6_ViralClipsNow and our TikTok account @gta6_viralclipsnow, and our internal applications "Viral Clips Now Mailer" (Google) and "Viral Clips Now Uploader" (TikTok) (together, the "App"). The App is used only by the channel team; it is not a public service and members of the public cannot sign in to it.

1. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our channel or the App, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access

  • Our own Google account (OAuth). The channel owner authorizes the App with Google OAuth. This gives the App access tokens for our own YouTube channel (to upload and schedule our Shorts, list our videos and read our channel statistics through the YouTube Analytics API) and for our own Gmail account (to send emails and read replies). We do not request or receive access to anyone else's Google account.
  • Our own TikTok account (Login Kit). The account owner authorizes the App with TikTok Login Kit, using the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload. The App receives our own TikTok account's open ID, display name and access/refresh tokens. It uses them only to confirm which account is connected and to send our own finished videos to our TikTok inbox as drafts through the Content Posting API; captions and publishing are completed by us in the TikTok app. We do not request or receive access to anyone else's TikTok account.
  • Public YouTube data about creators. Through the YouTube Data API we read public information about GTA 6 gameplay videos and their channels: channel name, channel ID, subscriber count, country and language when public, video titles and publication dates, and a contact email address only when the creator has published it.
  • Emails exchanged with creators. When we ask a creator for permission to use their gameplay, we keep the email we sent, their reply and the result (permission granted, conditions, or refusal).

We do not use cookies or trackers on this website, and we do not collect information about website visitors.

3. How we use it

  • To publish and schedule Shorts on our own YouTube channel.
  • To send our own finished videos as drafts to our own TikTok account.
  • To measure how our own Shorts perform.
  • To contact gameplay creators, ask for permission and keep a written record of permissions, credit requirements and removal requests.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Gmail data – Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to send permission requests and to read creators' replies to them. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to others, and is not read by humans except the channel team handling those permission emails.

5. TikTok

Our use of TikTok is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy. TikTok data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with third parties.

6. Service providers

To produce our Shorts we use a small number of processors under their own terms: Anthropic (AI assistance to select clip moments and write voice-over scripts; it receives video frames from gameplay creators who granted permission, not personal account data), ElevenLabs (text-to-speech for the voice-over script) and our server host in the European Union (Contabo). Google and TikTok OAuth tokens and permission records are stored only on our server, with restricted access.

7. Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens are stored on our private server in the European Union and are deleted when we stop using the App or when access is revoked.
  • Public channel information and permission records are kept while we may use that creator's clips, and for up to 24 months after the last contact so that we can prove permission.
  • Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, except for the records we must keep to prove a permission.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Creators can ask us at any time to remove their clips (we do so within 48 hours) and to delete their contact data and our email history with them.
  • You can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to teamvillogor01@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.
  • Anyone who has authorized the App can revoke its access at any time from the Google security settings page or, for TikTok, in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions. TikTok data we hold (open ID, display name and tokens) is deleted when access is revoked or when we stop using the App.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU).

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
