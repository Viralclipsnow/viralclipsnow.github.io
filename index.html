About the channel

Viral Clips Now (@GTA6_ViralClipsNow) is a fan-made YouTube channel that publishes short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) with funny fails, stunts and epic moments from GTA 6 gameplay.

Every clip comes from a gameplay creator who has given us written permission. Each Short shows the creator's name on screen and links to their channel in the description, so our viewers can discover them. If a creator asks us to remove a video, we remove it within 48 hours.

Our application

We run a small internal tool, "Viral Clips Now Mailer", used only by the channel team. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it. It uses Google APIs to:

  • YouTube Data API: find GTA 6 gameplay videos and their public channel information, upload our own Shorts to our own channel and schedule them, and read the list of videos on our channel.
  • YouTube Analytics API: read the statistics of our own channel (views, watch time) to learn which Shorts work best.
  • Gmail API: send permission requests to creators from the channel's own Gmail address and read their replies, so that we keep a record of every permission.

We only access our own YouTube channel and our own Gmail account. We never access, upload to or change anyone else's account. See our Privacy Policy for details.
